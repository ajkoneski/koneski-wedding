Getting There — Airports

Skopje International Airport (SKP)

Primary

The primary airport, located approximately 25 minutes (23 km) by taxi or private transfer from Skopje city center. Direct flights are available from major European hubs.

Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD)

Things to Do in Macedonia

If you arrive a few days before or stay on after the wedding, here are four favorite places to explore across Skopje and North Macedonia.

UNESCO Gem ~2.5–3 hrs from Skopje

Lake Ohrid

One of Europe's deepest and oldest lakes, famous for crystal-clear waters, cobblestone old town lanes, lakeside cafes, and historic churches overlooking the water.

Historic Stroll Central Skopje

Old Bazaar of Skopje

Wander through centuries-old cobbled alleys lined with traditional craft shops, bakeries, courtyards, and lively spots for Turkish coffee and local specialties.

Nature Escape 30 min from Skopje

Matka Canyon

Surrounded by dramatic cliffs along the Treska River, Matka is ideal for a scenic boat ride, kayaking, cliffside walking trails, or a quiet lunch by the water.

City Heart Walking Distance

Center of Skopje

Stroll across the Stone Bridge and Macedonia Square, explore riverside cafes along the Vardar River, and enjoy lively evenings in the heart of the city.
